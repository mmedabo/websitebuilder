 For Islamic Bank of Thailand, extra holidays are given for Eid al – Fitr Day (Ramadan Day), Eid al – Adha Day (Raya Hajji Day) announced by the Sheikhul Islam Office if the date does not fall on any of holidays listed above or Saturday and Sunday. 

 For financial institutions and Specialized Financial Institutions branches in Narathiwat, Pattani, Yala, Satun, and Songkhla, extra holidays are given for Eid al – Fitr Day (Ramadan Day), Eid al – Adha Day (Raya Hajji Day) announced by the Sheikhul Islam Office, and the Chinese New Year Day if the date does not fall on any of holidays listed above or Saturday and Sunday

Remark:
The cancellation of financial institutions and Specialized Financial Institutions’ holidays: 
   1. The Mid-Year Closing Day holiday (1st July) is cancelled from B.E. 2562 (2019) onwards.
   2. For Bank for Agriculture and Agricultural Cooperatives, the additional holiday (1st April) is cancelled from B.E. 2565 (2022) onwards.
   As financial institutions and Specialized Financial Institutions now have more efficient financial statement systems, which can be operated without affecting the services on all channels of the institutions, it is not necessary to prescribe as financial institutions and Specialized Financial Institutions’ holidays anymore.
